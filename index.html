Hive Kids Bible

Bible stories for young children, from Creation to the Day of Pentecost, with King James Version key verses to learn through short games, including who Jesus is (John 1:1, 14) and what we should do (Acts 2:38): fill in the missing word, tap the words in order, and say it from the first letters. Preview · For little readers, ages 3 to 6

Hive Phonics

Letter sounds from A to Z with a picture word for each, a bee that slides sounds together to read words like c-a-t, and a "which sound does it start with?" game. Big buttons for small hands, no accounts and no ads, and stars stay on your device.
